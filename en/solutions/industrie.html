Spare parts & industry: search that understands your references

A 6204-2RS bearing and a 6204-ZZ bearing share the same number. They are not interchangeable.

The problem, specifically in this industry

In technical parts, the suffix carries the essential meaning: seal type, pressure class, nominal diameter. A text engine sees a two-character variation and treats the references as equivalent. Your buyer, meanwhile, fits the wrong part on a production machine.

What Heurix understands in your references

The industrie rule pack activates when you pick this industry, at indexing time. It produces these annotations, each becoming a usable filter:

The trap a generalist engine falls into

The same part is ordered under four spellings: “6204 2RS”, “6204-2RS”, “6204RS”, “6204 2 RS”. Without structure recognition, each spelling returns a different result — and your catalog looks incomplete when it isn't.

Why the pack changes the outcome

The industry pack separates the number from the sealing variant, letting you both find the exact reference and knowingly suggest neighboring variants. A generalist engine doesn't distinguish the two: it either merges them or misses them.
