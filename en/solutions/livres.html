Books & publishing: search that understands your references

Two editions of the same novel share the same title and have two different ISBNs. Your customers sometimes search for one, sometimes the other.

One title, four distinct products

A bookstore catalog stacks several axes — title, author, genre, format, edition, language (the detail of each attribute). The same work exists in paperback, hardcover, collector's, and audio: four products, four prices, one title. Indexing just one of these axes misses half the searches.

ISBN, format and edition untangled

The livres rule pack activates when you pick this industry, at indexing time. It produces these annotations, each becoming a usable filter:

AnnotationWhat it isolates
ISBN_9782070413119the isolated ISBN, with or without hyphens
FORMAT_POCHEthe edition format
GENRE_POLARthe genre
ED_COLLECTORthe special edition
LANG_ENthe book's language

Three forms of one identifier

One customer types an ISBN with hyphens, another without, a third pastes an ISBN-10 while you index in ISBN-13. Three forms of the same identifier, and any one of them should be enough to find the book.

The same title, the right edition

The books pack recognizes the ISBN regardless of punctuation. It also cross-references genre, format, and edition, letting you offer “the same title in paperback” instead of an empty result on an out-of-print edition.
