A demotion always wins over a boost — a product explicitly hidden never resurfaces via a broader attribute rule.

Three things to know before your first import

  1. The identifier matters most. It's what lets a product be updated instead of duplicated. Re-importing the same file duplicates nothing.
  2. Use the same identifier everywhere. If your site sends product references to the conversion tracker, index with those same references — otherwise your sales analytics will stay empty.
  3. The rule pack is chosen up front. We analyze it from your file and preselect it. Changing it afterward requires re-importing.

Drop your ERP or PIM export, in CSV or XML. We detect the structure and suggest a field match — which you can correct before sending.

How it works

What a rule pack does

A rule pack reads your product references and automatically recognizes what they contain — as in the diagram above. Ten packs currently exist, covering the most common industries (hardware, fashion, industry, books, electronics, wine & spirits, automotive, electrical, plumbing, finance).

Create a custom rule

Possible directly from each catalog card, with no code to write — two formats to choose from. Here's exactly what to fill in:

Example — Keyword → label

Your customers type "anchor," others "molly" or "drywall anchor" for the same product.

TypeKeyword → label
Rule nameAnchor
Equivalent wordsanchor, molly, drywall, drywall anchor

→ A search on any of these words finds every product containing any of the other three.

Example — Prefix + number → label

Your references use a standardized code, like a RAL color.

TypePrefix + number → label
Rule nameRAL color
Prefix to recognizeRAL

→ "RAL 9010" and "RAL9010" (with or without a space) are now recognized as the same value.

More examples by industry (hardware, fashion, beauty, furniture…): see the dedicated blog article.

More complex cases (two combined numbers, like a diameter × a length) aren't covered by these two formats yet. Need that? Write to us — it's doable on the engine side.

Publishing a change

There's no separate publish step: changing a rule pack, editing a synonym, or creating a custom rule takes effect immediately on your live search, as soon as you confirm.
