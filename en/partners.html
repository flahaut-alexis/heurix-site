Partner program

The partner program is opening

Heurix is looking for its first partner agencies. The terms are not written yet: they will be set with whoever joins the program first. This page says where things stand today, and stops where the undecided begins.

What this program cannot promise you

An established vendor's partner program usually comes with a dedicated implementation manager, a training catalog, a certification track, and a page covered in the logos of agencies already signed.

Heurix can deliver none of that. The engine is built by one person. There is no training team to call on, no certification to pass, no implementation manager to assign to you. And there are no logos to display: the program is opening, and you would be among the first.

Writing the opposite would be easy. It would show on the first call.

What there is instead

One thing, and it is the only one a company this size can offer: you talk directly to the person who writes the engine.

What that changes, concretely. If one of your clients searches by a reference format the industry pack does not recognize, the rule can be added. Not a feature request filed in a queue, not a ticket escalated to a product team: the rule cascade is the product, and extending it is the daily work.

It is an advantage that fades as a team grows. It exists today.

Implementation time

It depends less on Heurix than on what you are replacing. The three orders of magnitude below are the ones in the integration documentation, quoted as they stand.

Autocomplete

The dropdown under the search bar, and nothing else.

1 day

List of identifiers

Heurix decides which products and in what order. The template, the product pages and the facets stay the site's own. Count 2 to 3 days on PrestaShop, 3 to 4 on WooCommerce.

3 to 5 days

Full replacement

The whole results page, facets and analytics included.

2 to 3 weeks

These figures come from the documentation, not from a brochure: it also records the cases where they do not work in our favor. The Magento 2.4+ results page goes through Elasticsearch, which puts it at the third level rather than the second. And Shopify is unverified to date — the documentation says so rather than offering an approximate recipe.

Who this program is for

Every application is reviewed. There is no minimum volume to justify and no tier to reach: a scale needs partners to discuss it, and that is exactly what is missing today.

Particular interest goes to agencies specialized in B2B e-commerce and industry. That is where searching by reference, by equivalence and by dimension raises problems keyword search does not solve — and two of the existing rule packs already cover that ground.

If your specialty lies elsewhere, write anyway: the engine knows nothing about sectors, only about reference formats.
