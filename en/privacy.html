Privacy policy

Last updated 26 July 2026. Written to be read, not to protect us.

Who processes your data

Heurix is operated by Alexis Flahaut, sole trader, 22 rue Mélingue, 75019 Paris, France — SIRET 107 681 900 00018. For any question about your data: contact@heurix.fr.

There is no data protection officer: the business does not meet the thresholds requiring one. Your requests reach one person, who answers them personally.

Two distinct situations, not to be confused

When you use Heurix as a customer, we are the data controller for your account: your email address, company name, billing details, and how you use the console.

When your visitors search your catalogue, we are a processor acting on your behalf. Their search queries pass through our engine, and you remain the controller. This distinction matters: it determines who answers if one of your visitors exercises their rights.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising purposes. We do not mine your catalogues for anything other than serving you. Your data is not used to improve the service for other customers.

The showcase site drops no tracker before your explicit consent — see the “Trackers and cookies” section below.

Something you should be aware of

Your visitors' search queries are recorded so we can show you your analytics. Most of the time these are product names. But a search box is a free-text field: a visitor may type an email address, a phone number, anything.

We do not filter this content — we cannot reliably tell a product reference from personal data. It is therefore your responsibility, on your own site, to inform your visitors that their queries are recorded, and to collect any consent required. Heurix cannot do this on your behalf, because we never see your visitors.

What we fixed. Until 26 July 2026, Google Tag Manager loaded as soon as you arrived on the site, without prior consent, and this page wrongly stated that no traffic-measurement tracker was used. That is no longer the case: no non-essential tracker loads before your explicit choice. We mention it because a privacy policy that hides its past corrections is not worth much.

Refusing has no consequence. The site and the client area work exactly the same. You can change your mind at any time through the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of every page.

Two external libraries are loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com to render charts and generate PDF exports in your console. They set no trackers, but loading them exposes your IP address to Cloudflare, as any request to a third-party server does.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correct it, have it erased, obtain it in a portable format, or object to its processing. Write to contact@heurix.fr; we answer within one month.

Erasure has one limit: invoices must be kept for ten years under accounting law. We can delete your account and catalogues, not your invoices.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. API keys are compared in constant time. Databases are backed up daily with integrity verification. Sign-in is rate-limited against brute-force attempts.

We state what is in place, not what is planned.

Complaints

If our answer does not satisfy you, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority — cnil.fr — or with the supervisory authority of your own country of residence.

Changes

Any substantial change is announced by email to account holders. Minor updates are reflected in the date at the top of this page.
